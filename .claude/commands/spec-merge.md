Compare the team's specs and move them toward one final spec. Follow `specs/README.md`.

Inputs:
- `specs/varnika.md`, `specs/devaj.md`, `specs/shannin.md`: the three personal specs. Never edit them.
- `specs/conflicts.md`: may already hold decisions the team made. Never drop or change a filled-in **Decision**.
- `docs/DESIGN.md` and `docs/sources/`: background only. They are not a vote, but quote DESIGN.md where it helps the team decide, and flag an option that contradicts the hackathon rules or the Hyphenate rubric in `docs/sources/`.

Steps:
1. If any of the three specs is still the empty template (only headings and comments), stop and say whose spec is missing.
2. Go section by section (the 14 headings). Break each section into individual decisions and match them across the three people by meaning, not wording.
3. Sort each decision into one group:
   - **Conflict:** two or more people say incompatible things. A `?` bullet counts as a position, marked unsure.
   - **Solo:** only one person mentions it. The team must decide keep or drop.
   - **Agreed:** everyone who addressed it says the same thing, and at least two people addressed it.
4. Rewrite `specs/conflicts.md` in this format. Give each item a stable ID (`C-<section>-<n>` for conflicts, `S-<section>-<n>` for solo items) and keep the IDs of items that already exist.

   ```
   # Spec conflicts

   Generated by /spec-merge. Fill in **Decision** and **Decided by** under each open item, then run /spec-merge again.

   Status: <n> open · <n> resolved · <n> agreed

   ## <section heading>

   ### C-4-1 <the question, in plain words> (OPEN | RESOLVED)
   - **Varnika:** <position, or "not covered">
   - **Devaj:** <position, or "not covered">
   - **Shannin:** <position, or "not covered">
   - **DESIGN.md:** <what the earlier draft says, if relevant>
   - **Note:** <only if an option breaks a rule or rubric item; cite the source file>
   - **Decision:**
   - **Decided by:**

   ### S-4-1 <item> (OPEN | RESOLVED)
   - **Proposed by:** <name>: <position>
   - **Decision:** keep / drop / change to …
   - **Decided by:**

   ### Agreed
   - <decision> (all who covered it: <names>)
   ```

   An item is RESOLVED when its **Decision** is filled in. If a personal spec changed after a decision was made and now conflicts with it, reopen the item and say what changed.
5. If no items are open, write `specs/final.md`: the same 14 headings, each containing the agreed items plus every resolved decision, written as plain statements with no names and no IDs. Start it with one line saying it was generated from conflicts.md and the date. If items are still open, leave `final.md` alone.
6. Reply with: the counts (open / resolved / agreed), the open items' IDs and questions, and whether `final.md` was written.
